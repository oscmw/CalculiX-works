About us

People contributing to this repo will mostly be affiliated with the loosely tied group of mechanicians entitled open-source computational mechanics and acoustics workshop, or OSCMW.

The “thread” called CalculiX-works was created in order to share information, experience, knowledge as well as models created in CalculiX.

This document will be organized as blog sections (threads) devoted to specific problems which will be found as issues (and branch names for that matter), in the github repo. For example, our first issue is related with the earing phenomenon in deep-drawing process. The second issue will be buckling, wind and earthquake response of the skirt of a vertical pressure vessel. Hopefully, there will be more topics (issues) to follow these…

The final section will be References where an enriched list of content addresses about CalculiX is aimed.

Skirt Response

In this problem buckling of a simplified cylindrical skirt model (the structure used to support a vertical pressure vessel) is studied under various types of loading such as static gravitational, wind, earthquake, etc.
